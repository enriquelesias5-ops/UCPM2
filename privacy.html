Privacy Policy

UTeM Confessions respects the privacy of our users. This policy outlines how anonymous and technical data is processed.

1. Anonymous Confessions

Our confession submission system does not collect personal identifiers, email addresses, phone numbers, or IP addresses. Submissions are processed as plaintext statements. To maintain absolute privacy, avoid typing names or specific identifiers in the body of your confession text.

2. Local Storage Usage

We utilize browser `localStorage` locally on your device to store your chosen visual theme configuration (Dark/Light mode) and a submission timestamp. The timestamp is used solely for browser-side anti-spam throttling (preventing submissions more than once every 30 seconds). No data is transmitted to our servers from local storage.

3. Third-Party Advertising and Cookies

We partner with third-party networks, specifically Adsterra and Monetag, to serve advertisements on our platform. These ad networks may use browser cookies, web beacons, and unique device identifiers to collect anonymous traffic statistics, measure advertising performance, and display tailored promotional material. You may manage or opt out of personalized tracking in your browser settings.

4. GPA & GPA Calculator Tools

Any subject details, credit records, or grades entered into the GPA and CGPA calculator widgets are processed locally on your client machine. This data is not stored, transmitted, or logged anywhere outside of your local browser execution window.
